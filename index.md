InnovationLet Finance is the finance desk of the InnovationLet network. We cover the plumbing that moves money, the companies building on top of it, and the rules that decide how far they can go. No stock-tip energy, no crypto evangelism. Just the structure, the incentives, and what the numbers actually say.

## What we cover

- **Markets**: listed companies, funding flows, and the earnings details that get buried in summaries.
- **Fintech**: payments, lending, banking infrastructure, and the margins underneath each.
- **Enterprise**: treasury, compliance, and the back-office software most people never see.
- **Digital assets**: stablecoins, tokenisation, custody, and the regulation catching up to all three.

## How we report

We follow primary sources first: filings, prospectuses, regulator notices, and company accounts. When we cite a claim, we link it. When the math doesn't add up, we say so. The desk publishes daily, with a longer brief each week for subscribers.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does InnovationLet Finance cover?**
Markets, fintech, enterprise finance, and digital assets. Every story is filed under one of those four areas.

**How often is it updated?**
The desk publishes daily during the week. Subscribe to the newsletter for a weekly round-up.

**Can I pitch a guest post?**

**Where can I find the other categories?**
